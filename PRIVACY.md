# Privacy Policy — Interval Timer

**Last updated: 21 August 2026**

Interval Timer is an interval workout timer for Android and Wear OS, published by Chris Poole.

## The short version

**Interval Timer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. The only network permission the app holds is
the one Google Play Billing requires in order to sell the one-time unlock, and the only thing that
ever crosses it is Play asking Play whether you have bought it — see **Purchases** below.

## What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored **only on your own device**, in the app's private storage:

- Your timer settings — work, rest and round counts, the "get ready" countdown, sound volume and
  mute state, the display language, the colour theme, and the Minimal toggle.
- Any interval sequences ("presets") you create, including the names you give them.
- Which built-in presets you have chosen to hide.

None of this leaves your device except in the one case described below. Uninstalling the app
deletes all of it. There is no cloud backup and no copy held by the developer.

## Purchases

Interval Timer offers one optional one-time purchase, which adds six extra colour themes and
removes the three-sequence save limit. It is not a subscription and there is nothing else to buy.

The purchase is handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. The app never sees or stores your name,
your email address, or any payment details — Google handles the transaction and tells the app only
whether this Google account owns the unlock. That check happens when the app starts and when you
tap Restore purchase, and it is the only network traffic the app generates. Google's handling of the
payment is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Nothing about your workouts, presets or settings is sent to Google, to the developer, or to anyone
else as part of this.

## Wear OS watch sync

If you have a paired Wear OS watch and install the Interval Timer watch app, the phone app sends
your preset names and their interval durations to **your own watch** so the same workouts are
available there. This uses the Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer, which is the standard
Android mechanism for phone-to-watch communication.

This data goes only to your own paired watch. It is not sent to the developer and is not used for
any other purpose. The transport itself is provided by Google Play Services and is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you do not use a Wear OS watch,
nothing is transmitted at all.

## Permissions, and why they exist

- **Foreground service / special use** — keeps your interval clock running accurately and plays the
  cue tones while the screen is off or you are in another app. Active only while a workout is
  running, and always shows an ongoing notification while it is.
- **Wake lock** — prevents the device sleeping mid-workout so the timer does not lose time.
- **Post notifications** — displays the ongoing workout notification. You can decline this; the
  timer still works.

The app requests no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, health data, or
device sensors.

## Advertising

Interval Timer currently contains **no advertising and no advertising SDKs**. If advertising is
added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the
Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated to match.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no user-generated
content, no social features, and no external links.

## Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will
also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
